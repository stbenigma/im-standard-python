Entity

Administrative Territory

**Territorial division of a country into smaller units.

According to NUTS (https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/NUTS), the country is at NUTS 0
NUTS 0 Nation State
NUTS 1 "major socio-economic regions (grouping basic regions)" ... major regions/areas of the country
NUTS 2 "basic regions (for the application of regional policies)" ... medium-sized regions, megacities
NUTS 3 "small regions (for specific diagnoses)" ... smaller regions, some of them already large cities
LAU 1 o.A. ... municipal administration associations, partly municipalities (not defined in all countries)
LAU 2 "municipalities or equivalent units" ... municipalities, partly municipal divisions

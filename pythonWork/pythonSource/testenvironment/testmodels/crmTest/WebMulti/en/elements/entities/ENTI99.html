ZIP-Area

**Territory allocated by the Post Office.
In many countries, the postal code is not a number but a string of letters and numbers

There are hierarchical systems for the structure of postal codes:
FOR EXAMPLE, USA
221 - 62 -10 -10
Segment - Sector - Post office or delivery section - routing zone or processing and delivery centre

There may be other standardised systems
P3: Postcode
P2: Postal code sub-area
P1: Postcode area
P0: Country
